Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

Welcome to PureSpace - AI Phone Cleaner ("PureSpace", "we", "our", or "us"). We believe that your digital life is strictly your own business. This Privacy Policy outlines our uncompromising commitment to your privacy and explains how our application functions without compromising your personal data.

1. 100% On-Device Processing

PureSpace is architected as a standalone, serverless iOS application. We do not maintain backend servers for data collection. Every core feature—including AI photo analysis, contact deduplication, and calendar scanning—is executed strictly within the local memory of your Apple device using native on-device machine learning frameworks.

2. Permissions We Request & How They Are Used

To provide our smart cleaning features, PureSpace requires explicit permission to access specific data on your device. This access is ephemeral and strictly localized:

  • Photo Library (Read & Write): We request access to scan for duplicates, burst shots, blurry images, screenshots, and to perform semantic categorization (e.g., identifying documents or landscapes). We analyze image pixel data locally to suggest cleanups. We never upload, hash, or transmit your photos or videos to any external server. Deletions only occur upon your manual confirmation.
  • Contacts: We request access to scan your address book to identify duplicate names/numbers and empty contact cards. This comparison happens in your device's RAM. We do not harvest, store, or share your contact lists.
  • Calendars: We request access to locate expired events and potential spam subscriptions. Your schedule and event details remain strictly on your device.

3. Zero Data Collection Policy

Because PureSpace operates entirely on-device, we are physically unable to collect your personal information. Specifically, we DO NOT collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (Names, Email Addresses, Phone Numbers).
  • Media files (Photos, Videos, Audio).
  • Device Identifiers, IP addresses, or precise location data.
  • In-app behavioral tracking data.

4. Subscriptions & In-App Purchases

All subscription payments are securely processed and managed by Apple Inc. via the native StoreKit framework (In-App Purchases). We do not collect, process, or have any access to your credit card information, billing address, or Apple ID details. We only receive an anonymous, cryptographically signed receipt from Apple to validate your premium status locally on the device.

5. Data Security

Your data is protected by the robust, hardware-level security of the iOS Sandbox environment. Because your personal files never leave your device through our application, they are not susceptible to data breaches, server hacks, or unauthorized third-party sharing from our end.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our app features or legal requirements. Since we do not collect user contact information, we cannot notify you individually. We encourage you to review this page occasionally. Any changes will take effect immediately upon being posted here.
