Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my PRO subscription?

If you reinstall the app or switch to a new iPhone, you can restore your subscription by tapping the "PRO" badge in the app and selecting "Restore Purchases" at the bottom of the screen. You will not be charged again.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To cancel, go to your iPhone's Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions > PureSpace, and tap "Cancel Subscription". Please make sure to cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

Is my privacy protected?

Absolutely. PureSpace operates with a 100% On-Device Privacy Guarantee. All photo scanning, video compression, and contact analysis happen locally on your iPhone. We never upload your personal files to external servers.
